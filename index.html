Design it once — send it twice.

  1. 1. Design — click equipment on the left to add it, drag it into place. Click any item to type its exact size and position in millimetres. Set your trailer's sizes and doors/windows in the right panel.
  2. 2. Factory — preview the full manufacturing drawing, then press ⬇ Factory DXF. That file opens in your supplier's CAD (ZWCAD/中望) with every measurement exact.
  3. 3. Customer — preview the clean concept drawing, then press ⬇ Customer PDF to send it for approval.
