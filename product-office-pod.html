Good questions

Office Pod FAQs

Do I need council approval for an office pod?

In many Australian council areas, structures under 10m² qualify as exempt development if they meet setback and height rules. Rules differ by state and council — we provide a checklist and help you confirm before you order.

How is it powered?

A weatherproof 15A inlet connects to your house via a suitable outdoor circuit (your electrician can install one in about an hour), or go fully off-grid with the solar package.

Can it double as a guest room?

Plenty of customers add a sofa bed. For regular guests, look at the Studio Pod — it adds a bathroom and kitchenette.
