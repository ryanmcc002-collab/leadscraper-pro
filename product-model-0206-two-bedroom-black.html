Good questions

Black Edition FAQs

What's different from the Classic White?

The layout is identical — the difference is the exterior and window package: 20mm feature cladding over the EPS panels, a black RAL 9011 frame and entrance door, and thermal-break aluminium windows with fly screens instead of plastic-steel.

Why choose thermal-break aluminium windows?

The frame has an insulating barrier between inside and outside faces, cutting heat transfer and condensation — a meaningful comfort upgrade in hot summers and cold mornings alike.

Is it suited to short-stay accommodation?

It's the finish we'd pick for it: the clad exterior photographs like an architectural cabin, and the black window frames give listings the look guests expect at premium nightly rates.
